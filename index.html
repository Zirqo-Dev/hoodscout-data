How to read this what each tier means

Tiers describe the market a token trades in, not whether it is worth owning. Numbers below come from forward tests over the collected history and are stated so they can be checked, not trusted.

PERSISTENT best in the data

Kept showing up healthy across at least 4 hours and holds real depth against its own FDV. Not best performing: over the next 4-8 hours these showed no edge over WATCH (paired within token: better for 30 tokens, worse for 42).

EMERGING emerging

Under 3 days old, trades not too concentrated, and buyer count rising across recent snapshots. Lottery-shaped: in past data the median 4-8 h return was about -7.5% but the mean was +1.5%, because a few big winners offset many losers.

WATCH watch

At least 300 trades in 24 hours, but short of a higher bar. Each row carries a reason for which bar it missed.

SPECULATIVE speculative

Thin, new or small, but with a venue to trade against. Kept rather than discarded, and the least reliable bucket to read: only about half of these had a price 4-8 hours later at all.

DEAD dead

No liquidity at all, or past 14 days old with under 10 trades in 24 hours. In past data none of these had a price 4-8 hours later, so there is nothing to measure and nothing to exit into.

AVOID avoid

Listed by hand with a reason. This tier overrides every other rule. A ticker is not an identity here: impersonators reuse them, so the contract address is what matters.

Daily routine

  1. Check the freshness chips are green.
  2. Look at EMERGING and Discover for names you don't recognise.
  3. Don't act on a name. Run the checklist.

Checklist before you care about any token

  1. Verify the contract address, not the ticker. Use the Gecko and DexScreener buttons on the row.
  2. Check liquidity against your own size. Can you get out?
  3. Check who is trading. Many different buyers is good; a few wallets trading back and forth is a warning.
  4. Check the source. Who made it, is anyone talking about it?
  5. Kill-test it. What would make this worthless? If you can't answer, you don't know enough yet.

Limits

It doesn't predict: high move scores tended to come before worse 4-8 h returns in past data. It can miss a fake it hasn't seen yet. It sees trading data, not whether the project is any good.
